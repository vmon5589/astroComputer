astroComputer

Before electronic machines, a "computer" was a person, often one of the women at Harvard College Observatory, who turned raw starlight into numbers. Learn their calculations one idea at a time, then reassemble them yourself.

① OverviewEach formula is explained step by step: what every variable means, why astronomers chose that formula, and the idea behind it.
② PracticeNo arithmetic. Each problem is a puzzle: choose the right formula tile from the tray, including some wrong ones, and place it in the correct slot.
③ Build upWithin a module, steps go from simple to complex. Later modules reuse earlier ones, so working in order works best.
